## Explanation

The principle that any identity (user, service, or workload) should hold
only the permissions it needs to do its specific job, for only as long as it
needs them — the design target that [capability-based authz](capability-based-authorization.md)
and scoped [workload identity](workload-identity.md) tokens are both aiming at.
