## Why this is catalogue only

As of 2026-10-24, AIB-C01 is in beta. AWS [announced it](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/training-and-certification/introducing-aws-certified-ai-business-strategist-built-for-the-people-who-scale-ai/) with beta registration from September 1, 2026, delivery from September 29, 2026, and an early-adopter badge for candidates who earn it by February 15, 2027. The [certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-business-strategist/) lists 85 multiple-choice and multiple-response questions over 170 minutes, a beta price of 50 USD (100 USD standard), Pearson VUE test centres or online proctoring, and English and Japanese. The [exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/ai-business-strategist-01/ai-business-strategist-01.html) states 130 minutes and a passing score of 700. That duration conflicts with the certification page, so confirm it before booking. The four domains are AI Fundamentals and Literacy (24%), AI Strategy and Business Value Creation (28%), AI Governance and Responsible AI Leadership (24%), and Business Readiness, Leadership, and AI Transformation (24%).

It is catalogue-only because it is a business-strategy credential and, per AWS, does not test knowledge of AWS services. The reader is moving into technical architect and agentic-AI roles, where AIF-C01 (AI Practitioner) and then AIP-C01 (Generative AI Developer – Professional) test the knowledge those roles need. AIGP covers AI governance in more depth.

## Differences from related exams

- **AIB-C01 vs AIF-C01.** Both are entry-level and contain no code. AIF-C01 tests AI and generative-AI concepts and the AWS services behind them, such as Amazon Bedrock and SageMaker. AIB-C01 tests use-case selection, business value, governance and organizational change, and does not test services.
- **AIB-C01 vs AB-100 and AIGP.** AB-100 is Microsoft's exam for designing agentic business solutions on its platform, so it is more technical and product-specific. AIGP (IAPP) goes much deeper into AI law, risk management and governance programmes than AIB-C01's governance domain.
