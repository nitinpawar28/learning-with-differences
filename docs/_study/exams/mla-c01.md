## Why this is catalogue only

As of 2026-10-24 the [certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-engineer-associate/) says the English version of MLA-C01 was available until September 28, 2026. The Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese versions remain available until MLA-C02 reaches general availability. This library records the final retirement as 2027-01-14, so confirm the date on the official page. MLA-C01 has 65 questions in 130 minutes and costs 150 USD. The MLA-C02 beta has 85 questions in 170 minutes, costs 75 USD during the beta and is in English only. Its beta delivery began September 29, 2026, and its general-availability dates are still to be announced.

For an English-language candidate the old version is already closed, so prepare for MLA-C02 (see its page in this library).

## Differences from related exams

- **MLA-C01 vs MLA-C02.** They test the same role: data preparation, model development, deployment and orchestration, and monitoring and security for ML workloads on SageMaker AI. MLA-C02's exam is longer, so expect more emphasis on newer and generative-AI-era MLOps. Use the MLA-C02 guide as your syllabus.
- **MLA-C01 vs AIF-C01 and AIP-C01.** AIF-C01 tests concepts with no building. AIP-C01 tests building generative-AI applications on Amazon Bedrock. MLA-C01 is about the classical ML lifecycle: feature engineering, training jobs, hyperparameter tuning, endpoints, pipelines and drift monitoring.
- **MLA-C01 vs DEA-C01.** DEA-C01 covers the data pipelines that feed ML, such as Glue, Kinesis, Redshift and Lake Formation. MLA-C01 starts where that data reaches SageMaker AI.
