## Why this is catalogue only

DP-600 is active as of 2026-10-24. The [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/fabric-analytics-engineer-associate/) lists a 100-minute proctored exam with renewal every 12 months, and the [study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/dp-600) shows skills measured as of October 19, 2026. It is an analytics engineer exam. Nearly half of it (45–50%) is preparing data: getting it, transforming it, and querying and analysing it with SQL, KQL and DAX. The rest is designing, building and optimising enterprise-scale semantic models (25–30%) and maintaining the analytics solution through security, governance and the development lifecycle (25–30%). That is a BI and analytics specialism, not part of your architect, agentic-AI, security or multi-cloud path. Architecture-level choices about data platforms belong in AZ-305 and this library's data architecture pages.

## Differences from related exams

- **DP-600 vs DP-700.** Both are Microsoft Fabric associate exams. DP-700 is for the data engineer: loading patterns, batch and streaming ingestion with SQL, PySpark and KQL, orchestration, and monitoring. DP-600 is for the analytics engineer working downstream of those pipelines, on semantic models and DAX.
- **DP-600 vs PL-300 (Power BI Data Analyst).** PL-300 is about reports and visuals for business users. DP-600 is about the enterprise semantic models and data preparation that those reports depend on.
- **DP-600 vs AWS.** AWS retired its Data Analytics – Specialty in April 2024. DEA-C01 (Data Engineer – Associate) is AWS's nearest exam, but it matches DP-700 better than DP-600.
