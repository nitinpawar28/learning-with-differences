## What the exam really tests

SC-401 is the Microsoft Purview data security exam. It sits on the Microsoft 365 side of the house: Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, endpoints, and the AI apps that read that data (Microsoft 365 Copilot and other generative AI services). The three domains are weighted equally at 30–35%:

- **Information protection.** Classification (built-in and custom sensitive information types, document fingerprinting, exact data match, trainable classifiers, OCR, Data explorer and Content explorer), sensitivity labels (roles, label design for items and containers, encryption and content marking, publishing and auto-labelling policies, labels in Defender for Cloud Apps), and protection outside the cloud (the Purview Information Protection client, the on-premises scanner, Purview Message Encryption and Advanced Message Encryption).
- **Data loss prevention and retention.** DLP policy design, roles, Adaptive Protection, rule precedence, Defender for Cloud Apps file policies, Endpoint DLP (device requirements, advanced rules, settings, just-in-time protection, monitoring), and retention (retention labels and policies, adaptive scopes, auto-apply, policy precedence and Policy lookup, recovering retained content).
- **Risks, alerts and activities.** Insider Risk Management (roles, connectors, Defender for Endpoint integration, indicators, templates, policies, forensic evidence, risk levels for Adaptive Protection, alerts, cases and notice templates), Purview Audit (Premium licensing, investigations, audit retention), Activity explorer, responding to DLP, insider-risk, Purview-in-XDR and Defender for Cloud Apps alerts, eDiscovery searches, and **protecting data used by AI** (Purview and Microsoft 365 controls for AI, and Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) for AI prerequisites, roles, policies and monitoring).

Scenarios are policy-design problems: "Finance documents containing customer account numbers must be encrypted and must not be shared externally, but HR must keep them for seven years." You choose the classifier type, label settings, DLP conditions and retention mechanism, and often their order of precedence. Points are lost on:

- **Labels versus policies.** Sensitivity labels versus retention labels; label policies (publishing) versus auto-labelling policies; retention policies (location-wide) versus retention labels (item-level).
- **Precedence rules.** The principles of retention (retention wins over deletion, longest retention wins, explicit wins over implicit, shortest deletion wins), DLP rule priority, and label priority order.
- **Classifier choice.** Regex/keyword SITs versus EDM (exact values from a database) versus fingerprinting (form templates) versus trainable classifiers (content shape).
- **Licensing and prerequisites.** Audit (Premium), Endpoint DLP onboarding, Insider Risk Management and DSPM for AI all have setup prerequisites that appear as "what must you do first" questions.

## Study strategy

1. **Foundations.** SC-900 domain 4 is the vocabulary list. Then read the Purview overview docs for information protection, DLP, data lifecycle management and insider risk.
2. **Classification first.** In a Microsoft 365 E5 trial tenant, create a custom SIT, an EDM schema and upload, a fingerprint from a template, and test them with Content explorer.
3. **Sensitivity labels.** Build a label taxonomy with encryption and marking, publish it, enable labels for containers (Teams, groups, sites), and create an auto-labelling policy in simulation mode.
4. **DLP and Endpoint DLP.** Create policies for Exchange, SharePoint/OneDrive, Teams and devices. Onboard one Windows device and test copy-to-USB and upload-to-cloud blocks.
5. **Retention.** Build retention policies and labels, an adaptive scope, an auto-apply label policy, and use Policy lookup to see which policy wins.
6. **Insider risk, audit and AI.** Enable Insider Risk Management with a data-theft template, run Audit searches, then turn on DSPM for AI and review its recommendations and activity for Copilot.

Official resources: the SC-401 self-paced learning paths and instructor-led course on the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/information-security-administrator/), the free practice assessment, the exam sandbox, the SC-401 exam prep videos, and the Microsoft Purview documentation linked from the [study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/sc-401).

How your background helps or misleads:

- **AZ-900 and AZ-204** cover Azure, not Microsoft 365 data. Very little carries over beyond Entra ID basics. Expect most of this exam to be new ground.
- **.NET background**: regular expressions for custom SITs and PowerShell (Security & Compliance PowerShell) will feel easy. Do not over-invest in the code; the exam is about policy behaviour.
- **Agentic AI interest**: the "Protect data used by AI services" group is the most directly relevant part for you. It is about labels and DLP being honoured when Copilot and agents read content, and about DSPM for AI giving visibility. That is a practical view of AI data governance, which complements AIGP-style governance theory.
- **AWS knowledge**: Amazon Macie (S3 data discovery) is the nearest AWS idea to classification, but AWS has no Microsoft 365-style label-and-DLP stack for documents and mail. The mental model does not transfer.

## Differences from related exams

- **SC-401 vs SC-900.** SC-900 describes each Purview feature in a sentence. SC-401 configures it, sets precedence, and investigates its alerts.
- **SC-401 vs SC-500.** SC-500 touches Purview only through DSPM for AI, SharePoint oversharing and Purview Audit queries from Defender XDR; its focus is Azure resources. SC-401 is Microsoft 365 data protection in depth. They meet on AI: SC-500's "Implement security for AI" group secures the AI platform (Foundry, API Management, Defender for AI services), while SC-401's "Protect data used by AI services" group secures the content that AI reads.
- **SC-401 vs SC-200.** SC-200 investigates Purview-sourced alerts as part of a SOC workflow; SC-401 creates the policies that generate them.
- **SC-401 vs SC-100.** SC-100 evaluates Purview as part of a Microsoft 365 and data security design. SC-401 is the implementation depth.
- **SC-401 vs SCS-C03 (AWS Certified Security – Specialty).** The AWS exam's data protection domain covers encryption with KMS, Macie, S3 controls and secrets management. It is infrastructure-centric. SC-401 is content-centric (documents, mail, chats). The closest overlap is "discover and classify sensitive data", and even there Macie and Purview differ greatly in scope.

## Exam-day notes

- 100 minutes; three equal domains, so do not let one difficult Insider Risk case study eat a third of your time.
- When a question lists requirements, sort them into classify, protect (label), prevent (DLP), retain and detect (insider risk or audit). Each maps to a different feature; options that solve two requirements with one feature are usually wrong.
- For retention questions, write the principles of retention on the whiteboard or scratch pad before you start.
- Watch for "simulation mode" and "policy tips" in options: the exam favours testing before enforcement when the scenario mentions minimising user disruption.
- Use current names: Microsoft Purview portal, Purview Information Protection client (not AIP client), DSPM for AI.
