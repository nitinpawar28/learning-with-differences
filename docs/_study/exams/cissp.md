## Exam format

All facts below are as of 2026-10-24. They come from the [CISSP exam outline page](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp/cissp-certification-exam-outline) and the [outline PDF effective April 15, 2024](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/internationf173-xmc4e73-prodbc0f-9660/media/Project/ISC2/Main/Media/documents/exam-outlines/CISSP-Exam-Outline-April-2024-English.pdf), unless another link is given.

- **Question types.** Multiple choice and "advanced innovative" items, as the 2024 outline PDF calls them. ISC2's [exam preparation page](https://www.isc2.org/exams/before-your-exam) says advanced items can include multimedia (charts and tables), calculation, order-response, drag-and-drop or hotspot, and scenario-based questions. None of them is a hands-on lab.
- **Adaptive delivery.** Every language version (English, German, Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese) uses Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT). Each answer changes the difficulty of the next item. The exam stops once the engine is statistically confident you are above or below the passing standard, so the number of items you see varies.
- **Number of items.** 100 to 150, with a 3-hour limit.
- **Passing score.** 700 out of 1000 scaled points.
- **Languages.** Chinese, English, German, Japanese and Spanish. The outline page says Chinese is offered only in set windows: March, June, September and December, each for the whole month.
- **Delivery.** Proctored at ISC2-authorized Pearson VUE test centres (PPC and PVTC Select). The [scheduling page](https://www.isc2.org/exams/schedule-exam) lists Pearson's fees: USD 50 to reschedule and USD 100 to cancel. An exam purchase must be used within 365 days.
- **Price.** USD 749 in the Americas on the [ISC2 exam pricing page](https://www.isc2.org/register-for-exam/isc2-exam-pricing). Prices vary by region and tax.
- **Retakes.** The [retake policy](https://www.isc2.org/exams/after-your-exam#retakePolicy) requires 30 test-free days after a first attempt, 60 after a second and 90 after a third or later attempt. You can make at most four attempts in 12 months.
- **Experience and endorsement.** Passing is not the same as being certified. You also need five years of experience (see the front matter) and an endorsement from an existing ISC2 member. Without the experience you become an [Associate of ISC2](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/associate).
- **Renewal.** The certification runs on a three-year cycle. You need 120 CPE credits per cycle (90 of them Group A) and an annual maintenance fee of USD 135, per the [member policies](https://www.isc2.org/policies-procedures/member-policies) and the [AMF overview](https://www.isc2.org/policies-procedures/amfs-overview). One AMF covers every ISC2 certification you hold.

**Coming change, effective 2027-04-15.** ISC2 has published a [new CISSP outline](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/internationf173-xmc4e73-prodbc0f-9660/media/Project/ISC2/Main/Media/documents/exam-outlines/2027/EXAMS-CISSP-Exam-Outline-English-RB-Revised-1016-ENU-2.pdf) that takes effect on April 15, 2027. The objectives above are the 2024 outline, which is live today. The main changes in the 2027 outline:

- Domain 1 is renamed **Security Governance and Risk Management**.
- The weights flatten: 13.6% for Domain 1, 12% for Asset Security, 12.8% each for Domains 3, 4 and 7, 13.6% for IAM, and 11.2% each for Assessment & Testing and Software Development Security. Domain 1 loses weight. IAM, Asset Security and Software Development Security gain.
- AI appears throughout. New items include "Ethics in AI" (1.1), AI threats such as adversarial AI, model poisoning and deepfakes (3.7), and zero-day attacks. HIPAA is added to the privacy and risk-framework examples.
- Domain 3 adds HSMs to the system security capabilities. The vulnerability list adds OT, supply chain risk, autonomous systems and quantum computing. Cryptography moves from "quantum" to "post-quantum".
- Domain 7 adds EDR/XDR, immutable backups, DR metrics (MTTR, MTBF), DDoS scrubbing services and "allowlist/denylist" wording.
- Domain 8 adds SSDF and SBOM requirements, shift-left and memory-safe languages.

Exam format, length and passing score stay the same. If you book on or after 2027-04-15, study from the 2027 PDF. Confirm all of this on the official pages before booking.

## What the exam really tests

CISSP is a management-level exam with a technical floor. It does not ask you to configure anything. It asks what a senior security professional should recommend, prioritise or do first, given the business's goals, the law and risk appetite. The famous mindset is "think like a manager or risk adviser, not a technician". Human safety comes first, then business objectives and governance, then technical controls.

The depth is a mile wide and roughly an inch deep, with pockets of real depth:

- **Risk management vocabulary.** Quantitative risk (SLE, ARO, ALE), risk treatments, control types and categories, due care vs due diligence.
- **Security models.** Bell-LaPadula ("no read up, no write down") vs Biba ("no read down, no write up"), plus the other classic models.
- **Cryptography.** What each primitive provides: confidentiality, integrity, authentication, non-repudiation. PKI trust chains and the key life cycle.
- **Networking.** OSI layers, protocol security, and segmentation from VLANs to micro-segmentation.
- **BC/DR.** BIA outputs (MTD, RTO, RPO), recovery site types and the order of DR plan tests.
- **Investigations and evidence handling.** Chain of custody and the types of investigation.
- **SDLC security and software assurance.** Maturity models, testing types, and the risk of acquired software.

Scenarios are usually short. A company plans a merger, a breach is discovered, a cloud provider is chosen, or a developer wants production access. You then pick the BEST, FIRST or MOST appropriate action. Several options are usually technically correct. The marks come from choosing the one that fits the role, the life-cycle stage and the governance order (policy before procedure, BIA before strategy, approval before action).

Candidates lose points in three ways. They answer as engineers ("patch the server") when the question wants governance ("inform senior management, follow the incident plan"). They over-read details that are not in the stem. They treat "most secure" as "best", when the best answer balances cost and business need.

## Study strategy

1. **Read the outline first.** Use the [2024 outline PDF](https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/internationf173-xmc4e73-prodbc0f-9660/media/Project/ISC2/Main/Media/documents/exam-outlines/CISSP-Exam-Outline-April-2024-English.pdf), or the 2027 one if you book after April 2027. Turn each bullet into a one-line definition plus one example in your own words.
2. **Foundations first.** Study Domain 1 (governance, risk, law, BCP) early. Every other domain is judged through its lens. Then do Domain 3 (architecture, models, crypto) and Domain 4 (networking), which hold most of the hard technical vocabulary. Domains 5 to 8 then build on familiar ground.
3. **Official ISC2 resources.** ISC2 offers [CISSP Online Self-Paced Training](https://www.isc2.org/training/online-self-paced/cissp-online-self-paced), online instructor-led training, classroom courses through partners, the free "Ultimate Guide to the CISSP", flash cards and a "Check Your Knowledge" quiz (all linked from the [CISSP page](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp)). ISC2 also publishes a list of [supplementary references](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/references).
4. **Hands-on is optional.** The exam has no labs. Still, sketching architectures helps a lot: a segmented network, a PKI trust chain, a federated SSO flow, a DR site plan. Explaining each sketch aloud is the closest thing to "hands-on" for this exam.
5. **Practise the CAT style.** You cannot skip or go back, so train yourself to commit to one answer per question.

**How your background helps or misleads.**

- **AZ-900** gives you the cloud service models and shared responsibility (3.1.10, 3.5.6). That is only a small slice of the exam.
- **AZ-204 prep** helps with Domain 8 and with identity in Domain 5: Microsoft Entra ID, OAuth 2.0/OpenID Connect, managed identities and Key Vault. CISSP asks about these generically ("federated identity", "credential management systems"), never by product name.
- **CKAD prep** helps with containerisation, microservices and serverless in 3.5.
- **Your .NET/Angular background** makes the SDLC, SAST/DAST and secure coding topics easy.

The trap is the developer reflex. You will want to fix things technically, but CISSP rewards the answer that goes through governance, ownership and documented process. Learn to ask "who owns this decision?" before "how do I fix it?".

## Differences from related exams

- **CISSP vs CCSP.** Both are ISC2 exams in the same CAT format with the same 3-hour limit and 700/1000 pass mark. CISSP covers the whole of security, with cloud as one item among many (3.5.6, 4.1.17). [CCSP](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/ccsp) goes deep on cloud alone: cloud data life cycle, multi-tenancy, CSP evaluation, cloud contracts, audit reports such as SOC and ISAE, and eDiscovery across jurisdictions. An active CISSP also waives the entire CCSP experience requirement.
- **CISSP vs ISSAP.** [ISSAP](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/issap) is a CISSP concentration for security architects. It has 125 fixed linear items, not CAT, and goes deeper into architecture modelling (SABSA, TOGAF, threat modelling), infrastructure security design and IAM architecture. CISSP asks what a control is. ISSAP asks how to design the system that combines controls.
- **CISSP vs CSSLP.** CISSP's Domain 8 is 10% of the exam. [CSSLP](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/csslp) spends a whole exam on the secure SDLC, from requirements and abuse cases to supply chain and decommissioning.
- **CISSP vs CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005).** CAS-005 is the closest CompTIA peer at the expert level. It is hands-on and engineer-focused, with performance-based questions. CISSP is managerial and scenario-driven, with no simulations. CISSP also has a hard experience requirement. CompTIA only recommends experience.
- **CISSP vs Microsoft SC-100 or AWS SCS-C03.** Vendor exams test a product stack: Microsoft Defender, Microsoft Entra and Sentinel, or AWS IAM, KMS and GuardDuty. CISSP is vendor-neutral and tests principles that apply to any stack. Vendor exams answer "which service?". CISSP answers "which principle, and in what order?".
- **CISSP vs ISACA AAISM.** AAISM is an AI-security management credential that assumes a CISM or CISSP-level base. CISSP is that base, and the 2027 outline begins to add AI governance topics.

## Exam-day notes

- Three hours for up to 150 items gives you about 72 seconds per item. CAT does not let you review or change earlier answers, so read every stem once, carefully, and commit.
- Watch the qualifiers: BEST, FIRST, MOST, LEAST, PRIMARY. They decide most questions.
- When two answers look right, prefer the one that protects people, follows policy, involves the right owner or senior management, or acts earlier in the life cycle.
- Do not panic if the exam ends at 100 items, and do not panic if it runs to 150. Neither tells you the result.
- Breaks are optional and the clock keeps running during them, but a short one can protect your focus in the last hour.
- ISC2 requires you to accept a non-disclosure agreement. Do not share or seek out live exam content.
