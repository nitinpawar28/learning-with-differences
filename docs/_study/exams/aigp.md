## About this certification

The **AIGP** is the IAPP's AI governance credential. The IAPP is the international association for privacy professionals that also runs CIPP, CIPM and CIPT. AIGP tests whether you can set up and run governance for AI systems across their life cycle, for both builders ("developers") and users ("deployers") of AI. AIGP is the IAPP's own designation, and there is no separate exam number.

### How the objectives above were built

The objectives transcribe the **AIGP Body of Knowledge and Exam Blueprint, version 2.1**, approved 9 September 2025 and **effective 2 February 2026** (it supersedes 2.0.1) ([BoK PDF](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltd4dd5b2d705252bc/blt0d33152fd20bc134/AIGP_Cert%20BOK.pdf)). The IAPP's structure is *domains* (I–IV), each split into *competencies* (I.A, I.B …), each with *performance indicators*. Here they become ids 1, 1.1 and 1.1.1, with the IAPP lettering kept in the titles and the performance indicators quoted as written. The weights are the blueprint's **minimum–maximum number of scored questions** per domain, in the IAPP's own form. The competency ranges are shown in brackets in the titles. The domain minimums add up to 77 and the maximums to 93, so the ranges describe the scored questions on an exam form rather than percentages. The IAPP reviews the BoK every year and announces changes at least 90 days before they reach the exam.

## Exam format

Facts are as of 2026-10-24, from the [AIGP page](https://iapp.org/certify/aigp/), the [IAPP store](https://store.iapp.org/aigp-exam/), the [certification FAQs](https://iapp.org/certify/faqs), the CPE policy and the [candidate handbook](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltd4dd5b2d705252bc/blteb8b5d531fd78971/IAPP-Certification_Handbook.pdf) (version 5.3.2, effective 1 June 2026). Confirm them before booking.

- **Question types:** multiple choice with one or more correct answers. The question says when more than one is correct, and multi-select questions tell you exactly how many to choose (for example "Select 3 of the 5 options"). You must pick exactly that number, and there is no partial credit (FAQ). Some questions are based on a scenario.
- **Number of questions:** 100 (store and FAQ). Some are unscored items that you cannot tell apart from the scored ones. The handbook says the scored and unscored counts are published on the designation page, and the IAPP's AIGP material has given them as 85 scored and 15 unscored.
- **Duration:** 2.75 hours (2 h 45 min) of testing. A 15-minute break is offered halfway. The break splits the exam into two halves with half the questions and half the time each, and you **cannot return to the first half** once you submit it, whether or not you take the break.
- **Passing score:** 300 on a 100–500 scale. The FAQ says the score depends only on how many scored questions you answer correctly, with no question weighting and no per-domain minimum, and that 300 does not mean 60%. After the exam you get a percentage-correct breakdown per domain.
- **Price:** USD 649 for IAPP members, USD 799 for non-members (first attempt, per the store). Retakes and additional IAPP exams are discounted. After a failed attempt you must wait at least seven days before the retake appointment (handbook).
- **Language:** English.
- **Delivery:** Pearson VUE test centres worldwide or OnVUE online proctoring. You must schedule at least 24 hours ahead and take the exam within one year of purchase.
- **Renewal:** a two-year certification term. Submit evidence of 20 hours of continuing professional education and pay the certification maintenance fee: USD 250 per term, or covered by IAPP membership at USD 295 a year (FAQ and [CPE policy](https://iapp.org/certify/cpe-policy) version 3.3.1, effective 7 April 2026). Retesting is the alternative to submitting CPEs.

## What the exam really tests

The AIGP is a **governance-professional** exam, not a technical ML exam. The BoK says its verbs (identify, evaluate, implement, define) signal question complexity, and that IAPP exam questions mostly sit at Bloom's *remember/understand* and *apply/analyze* levels. Expect:

- **Life-cycle thinking.** Which control applies at which stage: use-case assessment, impact assessment, data governance, training and testing, release readiness (model cards, conformity), monitoring, incidents and decommissioning.
- **Role thinking.** Obligations depend on whether the organisation is a developer or provider, a deployer, an importer or a distributor. Many questions turn on spotting the role in the scenario.
- **Law in outline.** The EU AI Act structure (risk tiers, high-risk obligations, transparency duties, general-purpose AI model duties, penalties), how GDPR-style privacy law applies to AI (lawful basis, minimisation, DPIAs, automated decisions), and how non-discrimination, consumer protection, product liability and IP law reach AI. The BoK also names the South Korean AI Basic Law and US federal and state AI laws as examples.
- **Frameworks.** The OECD AI Principles, the NIST AI RMF (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) and its Playbook, and ISO/IEC 22989 (terminology), 42001 (AI management system) and 42005 (AI system impact assessment).
- **Deployment judgement.** Assessing vendor contracts, choosing between model types and deployment options (fine-tuning vs. RAG vs. agentic), and planning for secondary use, external communication and the ability to switch a system off.

**Where candidates lose points:** answering as an engineer (the "best" technical fix) instead of a governance lead (the most defensible process step); mixing up EU AI Act provider vs. deployer duties; confusing NIST AI RMF functions; and running short of time in the first half, which is locked once submitted.

## Study strategy

1. **Start with the BoK.** Print the [BoK](https://assets.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltd4dd5b2d705252bc/blt0d33152fd20bc134/AIGP_Cert%20BOK.pdf) and treat every performance indicator as a question you must be able to answer.
2. **Learn the primary frameworks.** Read the NIST AI RMF 1.0 and its Playbook (free from NIST), the OECD AI Principles, and the structure of the EU AI Act (titles, risk tiers, Annex III high-risk areas, general-purpose AI model obligations). Read summaries of ISO/IEC 42001 and 42005. The standards themselves are paid.
3. **Use the IAPP's own resources.** The [AIGP training](https://iapp.org/train/aigp-training) (official course, online or live), the free AIGP study guide ([request form](https://iapp.org/l/aigp-study-guide-request)), the [free study guides page](https://iapp.org/certify/free-study-guides), the [how to prepare](https://iapp.org/certify/how-to-prepare) guidance, and the IAPP's official practice exam in the store.
4. **Work through scenarios.** For one AI use case, for example an internal RAG assistant on Azure OpenAI or Amazon Bedrock, write the impact assessment, the risk register, the vendor-contract checklist, the monitoring plan and the incident playbook. This covers most of Domains III and IV.
5. **Time-box the law.** About 19–23 questions come from Domain II. Learn structures and obligations, not article numbers.

**How your background helps or misleads**

- *AZ-900 and the Azure and AWS AI tracks* help with Domain IV deployment options (cloud vs. edge, RAG, agents) and with recognising technical controls. The exam wants the *governance* answer: who decides, what is assessed, what is documented.
- *Developer instincts* (.NET/Angular, AZ-204) can make you pick testing or monitoring answers when the question is really about policy, accountability or contracts.
- *Security background* (SC-900 and the security track) maps well to threat modelling, red teaming and incident management, but AIGP "risk" includes harms to individuals and society (bias, discrimination, autonomy), not only confidentiality, integrity and availability.

## Differences from related exams

- **AIGP vs PECB ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer.** AIGP is broad: law, frameworks and life-cycle governance across jurisdictions. The PECB credential is deep on *one standard*. It covers how to plan, implement, operate, measure and certify an ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system (clauses 4–10, Annex A controls, Statement of Applicability, internal audit, management review and certification audit). AIGP mentions ISO/IEC 42001 as one of three ISO standards. PECB builds the whole exam around it, open book, with three-option questions.
- **AIGP vs ISACA AAIA, AAIR and AAISM.** The ISACA AI credentials come from the audit, risk and security-management side and require existing ISACA or equivalent certifications. AIGP has no prerequisite and puts more weight on law, privacy and responsible-AI principles.
- **AIGP vs ISC2 and CompTIA security exams (CISSP, SY0-801, CY0-001).** Security exams treat AI as one more asset to protect. AIGP treats AI as a socio-technical system whose risks include fairness, transparency and accountability.
- **AIGP vs SWARC4AI (CPSA-A module) and the agentic-architect exams (Claude Certified Architect – Professional, PAA, NCP-AAI).** These test how to *build* safe AI systems: guardrails, evaluation and human-in-the-loop. AIGP tests how to *govern* them: policy, assessment, oversight and disclosure. The Claude Professional exam's Domain 5 (Governance, Safety & Risk Management, 14%) is the closest technical counterpart to AIGP Domains III and IV.
- **AIGP vs CIPP/E and CIPM.** The privacy credentials cover the data-protection law that AIGP Domain II.A summarises. Holders of those find Domain II easier, but Domains III and IV are AI-specific.

## Exam-day notes

- **Two locked halves:** about 50 questions in about 82 minutes each, roughly 1.6 minutes per question. Review flagged items *before* submitting the first half, because you cannot come back.
- **Answer everything.** Only correct answers to scored questions count, so a blank can never help you.
- **Look for the role and the life-cycle stage** in every scenario. Ask yourself "who is the provider or deployer, and is this design, training, release or use?"
- **Prefer process over tools.** When two answers look right, the one that adds accountability, documentation or an assessment usually beats the purely technical fix.
- **Multi-select:** the question tells you how many answers to choose. Choose exactly that many, because there is no partial credit.
- **Online:** the "Begin" button for OnVUE check-in appears in MyIAPP 30 minutes before your appointment. Use a personal computer (not a work-issued device), no VPN, and run the system check beforehand (handbook).
