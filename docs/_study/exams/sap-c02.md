## Why this is catalogue only

As of 2026-10-24 the [certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-professional/) says November 16, 2026 is the last day to take SAP-C02. Registration for its successor, SAP-C03, opens October 27, 2026. The current exam has 75 multiple-choice or multiple-response questions in 180 minutes, costs 300 USD, is delivered at Pearson VUE test centres or online, and is valid for 3 years.

Professional-level preparation takes months, and this exam closes in about three weeks. Plan for SAP-C03 instead (see its page in this library), after SAA-C03. The design patterns from SAP-C02 (multi-account organizations, hybrid networking, migration, cost and reliability trade-offs at scale) will carry over to SAP-C03.

## Differences from related exams

- **SAP-C02 vs SAP-C03.** They test the same role. Expect the new version to rebalance its domains and update its service list. Use the SAP-C03 exam guide, not SAP-C02 material, as your syllabus.
- **SAP-C02 vs SAA-C03.** SAA-C03 asks you to choose a suitable architecture for one workload. SAP-C02 asks you to choose the best of several plausible designs for an organization with many accounts, and its long scenarios ask you to trade off cost, migration effort and operations.
- **SAP-C02 vs AZ-305.** Both are design exams that test no implementation steps. AZ-305 is an expert-level Azure exam that requires AZ-104 first. SAP-C02 has no prerequisite, but it has longer questions and goes deeper into multi-account governance and migration.
