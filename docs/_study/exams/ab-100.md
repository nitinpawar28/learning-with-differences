## Why this is catalogue only

AB-100 is live as of 2026-10-24, so this entry is a deliberate skip, not a retirement. On the [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/) it is an expert-level credential: you must pass AB-100 *and* hold one of 13 associate certifications. Eight of the 13 are Dynamics 365 credentials (MB-230, MB-310, MB-330, MB-500, MB-800, MB-820, AB-210, AB-250) and one is Power Platform Developer (PL-400). The remaining four are AI-103 (Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer), AI-300 (Machine Learning Operations Engineer), AB-620 (AI Agent Builder) and AB-410 (Intelligent Applications Builder).

The exam is about AI inside Microsoft's business applications. It covers Copilot Studio agents, topics and agent flows; Copilot features in Dynamics 365 apps; extending Microsoft 365 Copilot; Power Platform; Microsoft Foundry models and tools; ROI and build-buy-extend analysis; and ALM, security and responsible-AI governance for agents. That makes it an architecture exam for Dynamics 365 and Power Platform consultants. Your agentic-AI and architect goals are better served on the Azure side by AI-103 and AI-500, with AZ-305 for general architecture. AI-103 is also one of the 13 prerequisites, so the path to AB-100 stays open if you want it later.

The [study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-100) already shows the outline that takes effect on November 16, 2026: Plan AI-powered business solutions (35–40%), Design AI-powered business solutions (30–35%), Deploy AI-powered business solutions (20–25%).

## Differences from related exams

- **AB-100 vs AI-500.** [AI-500](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/ai-500/) (Designing and Implementing Multi-Agent AI Solutions, for the Multi-Agent AI Solutions Expert credential) is code-first. It expects Python, Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Agent Framework, MCP, RAG and LangGraph on Azure. AB-100 asks the same question, how to compose agents into a solution, from the business-application side: prebuilt agents in Dynamics 365, Copilot Studio agents and agent flows, and Microsoft 365 Copilot extensions. It adds ROI and build-buy-extend decisions.
- **AB-100 vs AZ-305.** AZ-305 designs Azure infrastructure: identity, governance and monitoring, data storage, business continuity, and compute and networking. AB-100 barely touches infrastructure. It designs agent behaviour, grounding data, ALM and responsible-AI controls.
- **AB-100 vs AWS.** AWS has no business-applications architect exam. AIP-C01 (Generative AI Developer – Professional) is the nearest professional-level AI exam, but it is an implementation exam built on Amazon Bedrock. AIB-C01 (AI Business Strategist) shares the business framing but is deliberately non-technical.
