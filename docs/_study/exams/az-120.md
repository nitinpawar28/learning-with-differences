## Why this is catalogue only

AZ-120 is active as of 2026-10-24. The [certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-for-sap-workloads-specialty/) lists a 100-minute proctored exam with renewal every 12 months, and the [study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/az-120) shows skills measured as of April 17, 2026. It is catalogue-only because it assumes deep SAP experience with SAP HANA, SAP Business Suite and SAP NetWeaver. You do not have that experience, and the target roles do not need it. The exam spends its time on SAP-specific work: choosing SAP-certified VMs, Azure Center for SAP solutions and the SAP Deployment Automation Framework, Pacemaker and STONITH clustering for HANA and SAP Central Services, Write Accelerator and Azure NetApp Files volumes, the LaMa connector, and integration with RISE with SAP.

The parts that transfer, such as landing zones, ExpressRoute, availability zones, Azure Site Recovery and backup strategy, are better studied through AZ-305 and the architecture sections of this library.

## Differences from related exams

- **AZ-120 vs AZ-305.** Both are design-heavy. AZ-305 designs for any workload. AZ-120 applies the same building blocks under SAP's certification and latency constraints, using proximity placement groups, Accelerated Networking and HANA storage layouts.
- **AZ-120 vs AZ-104.** AZ-104 is the general administrator foundation that Microsoft recommends before AZ-120. AZ-120 adds Linux high-availability clustering and SAP lifecycle tasks that AZ-104 never touches.
- **AZ-120 vs AWS.** AWS retired its SAP on AWS – Specialty certification in April 2024, according to the [AWS announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/training-and-certification/aws-certification-retirements-and-launches/), so there is no current counterpart.
