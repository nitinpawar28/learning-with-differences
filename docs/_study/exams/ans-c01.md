## Why this is catalogue only

As of 2026-10-24 the [certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-advanced-networking-specialty/) says the exam is being retired. The last day to take it is December 31, 2026, and no new Advanced Networking – Specialty certifications will be issued after that. AWS names no replacement exam and points networking learners to digital training on AWS Skill Builder instead. Starting a specialty-level study plan about ten weeks before the exam disappears is a poor bet.

For reference, the [exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/advanced-networking-specialty-01/advanced-networking-specialty-01.html) describes 65 questions (50 scored, 15 unscored), 170 minutes, 300 USD and a minimum scaled score of 750. Its domains are Network Design (30%), Network Implementation (26%), Network Management and Operation (20%), and Network Security, Compliance, and Governance (24%).

The networking depth it tested still matters for the architect track. Hybrid connectivity, routing between many VPCs and accounts, DNS across environments and network security at scale come back at architect depth in SAA-C03 and SAP-C03, and on the Azure side in AZ-700.

## Differences from related exams

- **ANS-C01 vs AZ-700 (Azure Network Engineer Associate).** Both cover similar ground: hybrid connectivity, routing, private access to platform services, DNS, load balancing and network security. AZ-700 is an associate-level Azure exam. ANS-C01 is an AWS specialty that assumes years of networking experience and leans into design at scale, such as Transit Gateway topologies across many accounts, resilient AWS Direct Connect designs and hybrid DNS.
- **ANS-C01 vs SAA-C03 and SAP-C02/SAP-C03.** The solutions-architect exams treat networking as one design dimension among many. ANS-C01 went several levels deeper into routing, packet flow and network operations, and it expected you to troubleshoot and automate networks as well as design them.
